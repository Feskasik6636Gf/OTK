# Laughing is illegal: in this town laughter keeps the town afloat above a swamp.
In this town, laughing is illegal. Laugh once, and you pay a fine. Every street pole has a laugh detector camera. All the clowns are in jail, and comedies play with no sound. My dad giggled at work once, and he's still paying off the loan. Our teacher checks us for laughs by tickling us. Even babies cry on a strict schedule.
My best friend Tootie is a smuggler. His great-grandpa was the town's last clown. Tootie carries jokes in his backpack, written on tiny notes. One note costs a dollar. A mother-in-law joke costs two. "Rock and roll, we're losing control!" He whispers, and I choke into my pillow.
But something's weird. Every year, the town sinks a little deeper into the ground. First the basements vanished, then the first floors. Mayor Pop says that's totally normal.
One day, Tootie got caught. They put him in the middle of the square and burned all his notes. And right then, the whole town sank another three feet. Water poured through the windows. People screamed.
I looked at Tootie, and he looked at me, and we got it. A hundred years ago, the town was built on a swamp, and only laughter kept it afloat.
The water reached our knees. Tootie climbed onto a barrel and yelled, "So Principal Fartan walks into an elevator..." I couldn't hold it. I burst out laughing. The ground shook and rose a little. Then Mom laughed. Then Dad, then the police. The whole square laughed so hard the windows rattled.
The town rose higher and higher. The fines were canceled. The clowns were set free. Now every pole has a sign: "Laugh or drown."
Only Mayor Pop never laughed. His house is the only one still underwater.
And Tootie still hasn't told me how the elevator joke ends.
