# Pip: the tiny monster bodyguard who grows when you believe in him.
In this world, every kid gets a monster bodyguard on their tenth birthday. It follows you everywhere and protects you from anything.
Tootie got a giant bear with three eyes. It carries his backpack. Eddie got a fire lizard. Nobody bullies Eddie anymore. Principal Fartan got a skunk monster. And honestly, that explains a lot.
I got Pip. Pip is the size of a potato. He has one tooth and hides in my hood when a bird flies by. The whole class laughed at us for a week. "Rock and roll! We're losing control!"
Monsters grow when their kid believes in them. Tootie's bear grew so big it sleeps on the roof. But Pip stayed tiny. Because I didn't believe in him. Not even a little.
Then one night, the sirens went off. The Devourer came out of the forest, taller than the school, made of black smoke and teeth. It swallowed the fire lizard in one bite. Tootie's bear ran away screaming. Every monster in town ran. Every single one. Except Pip.
He climbed out of my hood, jumped to the ground, and stood in front of me. Shaking, tiny, alone. The Devourer looked down at him and laughed. And Pip squeaked the bravest squeak I've ever heard.
That's when I understood. He was never scared for himself. He was scared for me.
So I believed in him. With everything I had, Pip started to grow. Bigger than a car. Bigger than a house. Bigger than the Devourer. He opened his mouth with one giant tooth and roared.
The Devourer ran back to the forest and never came back.
The next morning, Pip was tiny again, asleep in my hood. Nobody laughs at us anymore.
