# The giant under the town: every night at 9 all lights go out, a giant sleeps below. Voice supplied by the user.
In this town, every night at 9, all the lights go out. No lamps, no TVs, no phones, not even candles. Because deep under the town, a giant is sleeping. If he wakes up, the town wakes up inside his stomach.
Everyone whispers. We walk on tiptoes. Dogs wear tiny socks. Our teacher speaks only in sign language after sunset. My dad sneezed once at night, and the whole street hid under tables for an hour.
My best friend Tooty is the loudest kid in town. He's grounded every night with a pillow in his mouth. "Rock and roll! We're losing control!"
Mayor Popp says the giant has slept for a thousand years, and we must never ask why.
But I hear something. Every night, right under my bed, a soft sound. Not snoring. Crying.
Last week, I couldn't take it anymore. I took my flashlight and sneaked down to the old cellar. Behind a rusty door, a tunnel went deeper and deeper. Tooty followed me, holding his breath so hard his face turned purple.
At the end of the tunnel, there was an eye. Bigger than a house. Wet. Red. We froze.
The giant whispered, and the whole cave trembled. "Why is it always so dark up there?"
I swallowed. "Because we're scared of waking you."
"I woke up long ago," he said quietly. "I just wanted to see your lights."
He wasn't afraid of the dark. He was lonely in it.
I turned on my flashlight and pointed it at his face. He smiled. A smile as wide as a valley.
That night, I convinced the whole town to turn on their lights. The windows glowed. Candles flickered. Tooty screamed with joy. And far below, the giant finally fell asleep.
Now the town is never dark, and every night, the ground hums a lullaby back.
