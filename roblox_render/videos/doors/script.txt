# Green door: hundreds of doors appear every night in the city. Mom came back wrong. Voice supplied by the user.
In this city, hundreds of doors appear every night, and every single one leads somewhere. By sunrise, they vanish. My friend Tarasic opened a purple door and found a beach with square waves. There was only one rule. Be back before sunrise. Otherwise, the door vanishes with you inside.
My dad calls the doors a trap. Mom didn't listen. A year ago, she walked into a green one and said, "Just a quick look." She came back at dawn, smiling, but something was off. She hummed songs I had never heard. She forgot I hate mushrooms. Dad watched her all through dinner. Then he whispered, "Rock and roll, lose control." Our secret code for run.
Last week, I finally searched for the green door. It appeared behind the school, glowing like a candy wrapper. "If you don't come back, I'll tell everyone you were brave." I stepped inside.
Darkness, then a kitchen. My kitchen. At the table sat my real mom, tied to a chair. "Run," she mouthed. Behind me, the door slammed shut. Mom laughed, but it came from the ceiling. Dozens of faces were pressed into the plaster, all wearing my mother's smile.
I cut the ropes with a butter knife. We sprinted through hallways that breathed. The window turned gold. We dove through the closing door just as the sun rose.
Dad was making pancakes. Mom squeezed my hand. "Listen carefully," she whispered. "Your father went through that door the night after me. He never came back."
The man at the stove turned around. He smiled. "Rock and roll," he said. "Lose control."
