# Back eyes: every mom has a second pair of eyes on the back of her head. Voice supplied by the user.
I spent a whole year trying to blind my mom. Then, her secret eyes saved my little sister's life. You see, in this world, every mom has a second pair of eyes on the back of her head. Real ones. They blink. They roll. They cry at movies. So moms see everything. Teachers face the board and still catch you passing notes. Moms drive forward and stare at the back seat at the same time. Barbers have to cut around them very, very carefully. And no kid in history has ever stolen a cookie. Ever. Except me. I tried every single day. I tiptoed. Blink. "Put it back." I crawled. Blink. "Put it back." I dressed up as the dog. Blink. "Put it back, Max."
So on my birthday, I made my wish. "Mom, I want privacy. Please cover your back eyes. Forever." She looked sad with all four eyes. But she put on a big wool beanie. And for a whole year, she never took it off. Best year ever. Cookies for breakfast. Video games at 3AM. Freedom!
But then we went to the beach. Thousands of people. Mom was buying ice cream, facing the stand. And my little sister floated away on her inflatable duck. Further. And further. Out to sea. I screamed, "Where is she?" Thousands of heads. Thousands of umbrellas. Waves everywhere. I couldn't find her.
Then I noticed something. Mom's beanie. Those eyes were made for exactly this. I jumped up and ripped it off. The back eyes opened for the first time in a year. They squinted in the sun. Blinked. Looked left. Right. Then they locked onto one tiny yellow dot, far out on the waves. "There!" Mom didn't even turn around. She ran backwards into the ocean, faster than any lifeguard, and swam straight to the duck. Splash! She brought my sister back. Safe.
That night, I threw the beanie in the trash. "Keep them open, Mom. Forever." All four of her eyes cried. The next morning, I reached for a cookie. Blink. "Put it back." Totally worth it. But then I found out dads have one eye on the back of their head too. It's just always asleep.
