# Never turn around when someone calls your last name.
In this town, you must never turn around when someone calls your last name. Your first name is fine. Your last name? Never. If you turn, the callers take you. No one has ever come back.
That's why teachers call us by first names only, and doctors just say, "Hey, you." At school, we write our last names in pencil and erase them after class. My dad got married without saying his last name once. The priest just pointed at him.
The callers live in the fog behind the old bridge. They can copy any voice. Your dog. Your teacher. Your best friend. My best friend Tootie turned around once, when he was six. His grandpa grabbed him just in time. "Rock and roll! We're losing control!" Now Tootie wears his hoodie backwards, just in case.
My mom always taught me one rule. "If you ever hear me call your last name, run. I will never do that."
Last winter, Mom went to the bridge to find our lost cat. She never came back. For a whole year, I didn't hear her voice, until tonight.
"Miller! Sweetie! It's me! I'm so cold! Please, turn around!"
It sounded exactly like her. Even her little cough. Tootie grabbed my arm. "Don't!"
"Miller, I found the cat. I just want to see your face."
My legs were shaking. Then I remembered her rule. Mom would never say my last name. I didn't turn. I walked home, crying. Behind me, the voice went quiet. Then it whispered, softer than ever.
"Good boy. That's my rule."
Tootie and I froze. It wasn't a caller. It was Mom. And she was making sure I'd never come looking for her.
