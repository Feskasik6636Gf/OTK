# Numbers: everyone has a number above their head that drops by one each midnight. Voice supplied by the user.
In this city, everyone has a number floating above their head, and it drops by one every midnight. Nobody can see their own. At zero, you vanish, and nobody ever says your name again.
Tarasik has 9,412 and brags about it. My mom's number is 11. "It's a mistake," she says. Every morning, I watch it fall. 10, 9, 8.
In the library, I found a torn page. "A number can be given. Press your forehead to theirs and say, take mine." That night, mom had two days left.
I pressed my forehead to hers and whispered the words. In the morning, above her head floated 400.
Mom woke up and screamed. "What did you do?" Tarasik stared at me and went pale. "Your number is one." Mom grabbed my face. "Take it back, right now!"
"I can't," I said. "A gift can't be returned." Then she stopped.
"Do you know why mine was 11?" she asked. I shook my head. "You were born sick. The doctors gave you three days. So every night since, I whispered those words to you. Your number was never yours."
Midnight was an hour away. Mom pressed her forehead to mine one last time. "Take mine," she said.
Tarasik gasped. Above my head, the number exploded into thousands. Above hers was zero. She smiled and kissed my hair. Then the clock struck 12 and the kitchen was empty.
Everyone says I must forget her name. So every midnight, I whisper it anyway. "Marina." And the number above my head has never dropped since.
