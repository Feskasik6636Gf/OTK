# Never open the door after midnight.
In this town, you never open the door after midnight. Not for friends, not for family, not even for Mom. Every house has three locks, a bell, and a sign. If they knock, it's not them.
The knockers come from the woods. They sound exactly like the people you love. Mrs. Dean opened the door for her husband once. In the morning, she was gone. Her husband had been asleep upstairs the whole time.
So, we sleep with earplugs. Even babies wear tiny headphones. My best friend Tootie says the knockers can't come in unless you open the door. "Rock and roll! We're losing control!"
Last night, at 3 a.m., someone knocked. Three slow knocks.
"Buddy, it's Dad! I went out to check the car and the door locked behind me. Let me in!"
I looked down the hallway. Dad's bedroom door was open. I could hear him snoring. So, it was a knocker. I didn't move.
The knocking stopped. Then, it started again. Faster. Different.
"Son, listen to me. Don't open the door. And don't wake the one sleeping in my bed."
My whole body went cold. The snoring in Dad's room stopped. Footsteps. Slow. Coming down the hallway.
"Buddy?" Said a voice behind me. Dad's voice. Sleepy. Kind. "Who's at the door?"
I didn't turn around.
Outside, the real Dad whispered. "It slipped inside when I opened the door. Run to the window!"
I ran. I jumped out and fell into the snow. Dad grabbed me, and we ran all the way to Tootie's house.
Behind us, our front door slowly opened. And something with Dad's face waved goodbye.
